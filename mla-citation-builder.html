MLA Citation Builder

Pick your source type, fill in what you know, and this assembles the Works Cited entry and the in-text citation in MLA 9 style. It runs on your computer.

What this does, and doesn't. It arranges your details into MLA 9 formatting. It can't check whether your details are right or complete, that's on you, so cross-check against the MLA guidance in this week's reading. Leave a field blank and it's left out. Nothing you type is saved anywhere but your own browser.
