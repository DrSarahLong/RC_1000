RC 1000 · Revision Tool

Revision X-Ray

Paste your argument or op-ed, then mark it up: highlight your claim, your evidence, and your counterargument in different colors. Check that every source is actually cited. Hear it read back. The tool doesn't grade you, it makes you find the parts yourself.

How this works. A checklist lets you say "yes, I have evidence" without proving it. This makes you point to it. If you can't highlight a claim, you don't have a clear one yet. If your evidence highlights are thin, that's your revision. Nothing here is graded or uploaded, it runs in your browser, and you do all the marking.
Private, saves only in your own browser.

1. Highlight the parts

Pick a color, then click sentences to paint them. Click a painted sentence again to clear it.

2. Check your citations

Every sentence you marked as a source or borrowed fact needs a citation or a link. Confirm each one.

3. Hear it

Reading aloud catches what your eyes skip, missing words, clunky rhythm, a sentence that drones. Do both if you can: let the device read it to you, then record yourself and listen for where you stumble or lose steam.

4. Note what you'll fix
