Schedule & Weekly Work

Sixteen self-contained weekly modules, done on your own schedule. Everything for a week is due that Sunday. Click any week to open its module. Exact calendar dates live on AsULearn.

Key deadlines

  1. Week 1: First-Day Writing (due Tuesday by noon on AsULearn, required)
  2. Week 3: Working thesis (Personal Essay)
  3. Week 6: Personal Essay due
  4. Week 7: Working claim (Argument)
  5. Week 9: Working source set
  6. Week 11: Argument Essay due
  7. Week 12: Op-Ed pitch + outline
  8. Week 13: Op-Ed due
  9. Week 14: Portfolio shell (published link)
  10. Week 16: Digital Portfolio due (culminating)

Your Writer's Notebook is collected twice for a completion grade: once at midterm and once at the end of term.
