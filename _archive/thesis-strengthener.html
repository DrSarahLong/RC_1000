Thesis Strengthener

Most writers get tripped up right here, at the working thesis. Paste yours and this will point out the common weak spots so you can sharpen it before you draft.

This is a first-pass thesis coach that runs on your computer. It flags common weaknesses, vagueness, announcing, clichés, no real tension, so you can fix them yourself. It can't tell whether your idea is true, or the right one for your essay; that's your call and mine. Paste, read the notes, revise, run it again. Nothing you type here leaves your computer.
