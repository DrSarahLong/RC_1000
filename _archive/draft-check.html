Draft Check

Paste your draft. It flags the fixable stuff a reader trips on, so you can clean it up before I ever see it.

This is a first-pass editor that runs on your computer. It catches common, fixable problems and known weak moves, typos and spacing, comma trouble, tired openers and clichés, vague filler, sweeping claims, quotes dropped in without a lead-in, and MLA slips. It will not scold your voice or your contractions, those belong in personal writing. What it can't do is tell whether your essay is good, has a real point, or moves a reader, so if it finds nothing, that means the surface is clean, not that the essay works. That judgment is still yours and mine. Fix what it flags, read it aloud, and make every paragraph earn its place. Nothing you paste leaves this computer.
