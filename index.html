The courseWhat Introduction to Writing and Inquiry is

This section of RC 1000 teaches writing and inquiry through the craft of narrative nonfiction and the lens of expressive, therapeutic writing. You write in three genres, then gather them into a portfolio you can add to throughout your career at App and share with future employers as proof of your communication and writing skills.

We write first to discover what we have to say, using expressive, invention-first techniques, then bring real craft (scene, voice, evidence, revision) to make the writing worth another person's time. The throughline all semester: writing is a way to think, feel, heal, and reach other people, and you get good at it because you keep it real.

By the end, you will have written:

  • A personal essay that turns a true experience into craft
  • An argument grounded in your life, widened with real sources
  • A public op-ed you could pitch to an editorial site your generation actually reads
  • A philosophy of writing and a reflective introduction
  • A published digital portfolio of your best work
